# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

People who want an aesthetically guided, personally editable crystal bracelet and may enter through AI preferences, Tarot reflection, or direct DIY creation. The primary use is consumer-facing on desktop and mobile web, including live demonstrations to partners.

## Product Purpose

Mystcrag turns a user's preferences or reflective prompt into three bracelet directions, then preserves user control through a visual DIY editor and order flow.

## Positioning

The product joins culturally framed design inspiration with a real, editable bracelet composition and catalog-backed fulfillment. AI and Tarot suggest visual directions; they do not make medical, guaranteed-effect, or deterministic-fortune claims.

## Operating Context

The main paths are AI inspiration, Tarot guidance, and direct DIY. Users review three recommendations, enter DIY, add, move, or remove beads, see price and wrist-fit guidance, save a design, and complete an immutable order.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Desktop and mobile responsive Next.js application backed by authenticated APIs.
- Crystal materials use catalog identity, authoritative price, wrist geometry, and 2.5D bead imagery.
- Tarot may recommend zero-stock active beads; the interface must disclose an estimated five-day replenishment wait without blocking completion.
- Raw inventory, supplier, cost, hidden prompt, and private Tarot state remain private.
- Existing routes, primary navigation labels, and core workflows must remain intact until a visual direction is explicitly approved.

## Brand Commitments

The product name is `玄矶 Mystcrag`. Existing user-approved references favor quiet warm-white space, restrained violet accents, refined Chinese typography, realistic luminous crystal beads, and a flat interface rather than a fully 3D scene.

## Evidence on Hand

- Current implementation and tokens in `app/globals.css`, `app/page.tsx`, and `src/features/tarot/`.
- User-approved Tarot draw and recommendation mockups stored in the current Codex generated-image history.
- Real product catalog and bracelet previews exist; testimonials, customer metrics, and guaranteed crystal effects must not be fabricated.

## Product Principles

- Make the next action obvious at every step.
- Keep recommendations explainable and editable.
- Treat fit and restock timing as clear guidance, not disruptive blockers.
- Let the crystal material and bracelet composition carry the visual focus.
- Preserve user trust through bounded cultural language and authoritative commerce data.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard navigation, visible focus, touch-sized controls, reduced motion, readable Chinese copy, responsive layouts, and non-modal errors or advisories are required.

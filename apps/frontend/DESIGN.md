# Mystcrag Atelier Design World

<!-- impeccable:design-schema 1 -->

## Direction

Mystcrag uses a contemporary jewelry atelier visual language: quiet ivory space, mineral gray typography, polished silver rules, and one restrained violet action accent. Crystal and Tarot imagery carry the color. The interface is editorial but never obscures the next task.

## Typography

- Display: Chinese Song/Ming serif for page titles, product names, and reflective reading titles.
- Interface: system sans for controls, prices, filters, guidance, and dense workbench information.
- Display titles use tight tracking and generous line height; small English labels are used sparingly.

## Surfaces

- Warm ivory is the canvas; paper-white is reserved for actionable surfaces.
- Thin rules and spacing establish hierarchy before cards.
- Functional surfaces use 8–12px corners. Pills are reserved for compact state, filter, or binary controls.
- Shadows are broad, low-opacity contact shadows, never generic floating-card shadows everywhere.

## Color

- Ink `#242027`
- Ivory `#f6f2ea`
- Paper `#fffdf8`
- Violet `#54386f`
- Soft violet `#eee8f2`
- Silver line `#d9d2c7`
- Amber is reserved for replenishment and operational advisories.

## Route Composition

- Home is photography/product-led with AI, Tarot, and DIY equally visible.
- Questionnaire focuses one prompt per viewport with a calm progress rail.
- AI and Tarot results show all three directions together on desktop and retain a visible next action.
- Tarot draw preserves the approved cream-and-violet fan composition and uses two rows when needed.
- DIY is a top-down 2.5D instrument: catalog, circular tray, selected beads, diameter controls, and authoritative completion summary.

## Responsive Rules

- Mobile is independently composed at actual CSS viewport dimensions; desktop canvases are never scaled down.
- Primary actions are reachable without horizontal scrolling.
- Touch controls are at least 44px.
- The mobile DIY tray and catalog share the viewport without stretching the bracelet stage.
